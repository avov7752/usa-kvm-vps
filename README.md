## The Routing Question: Which Network Line Actually Matters?

This is where most "美国高性能VPS" reviews get hand-wavy. Let's be specific.

### CN2 GIA (AS4809) — The Gold Standard for China Telecom

CN2 GIA is China Telecom's premium international transit path. Traffic rides the 59.43 backbone nodes end-to-end. If your users are primarily on China Telecom, this is what you want. The Los Angeles AMD Optimised VPS line includes CN2 GIA alongside 9929 and CMIN2.

### CU9929 — China Unicom's Premium Route

AS9929 is China Unicom's answer to CN2 GIA. Similar concept: premium transit with lower latency and less congestion than standard China Unicom international paths. Available on the AMD Optimised, AMD VPS, Intel, Ryzen9, and ISP lines.

### CMIN2 — China Mobile International Network 2

China Mobile's premium international backbone. If your user base skews mobile-heavy (which, in China, it probably does), CMIN2 matters. Bundled with 9929 on most LA China-optimized lines.

### International Network — For Everyone Else

The Global VPS and VDS lines use standard international transit. No special China routing. But you get 1Gbps ports and generous bandwidth caps, which is exactly what you want for non-China workloads.

> **The practical rule of thumb**: If any meaningful portion of your traffic comes from China, pick a China-optimized line (AMD Optimised, AMD VPS, Intel, Ryzen9, or ISP). If your audience is primarily elsewhere, the Global VPS line at 1Gbps is probably a better value.
